Beyond scheduling, the platform includes a comprehensive suite of marketing tools. The Dashboard provides performance overviews through uploaded analytics data, visual graphs, insights, and report generation. The Reports section allows users to generate marketing reports and compare performance across different campaigns or time periods. The Strategy section includes tools such as Brand Scorecards, ICP Builder, and Positioning Wizard to help businesses define their market strategy. The Brand section offers Brand Voice Builders and Tagline Generators to maintain consistent branding. The Campaign section includes tools such as Ad Copy Generators and Creative Brief Builders, while the Content Lab provides content creation tools including Ghostwriter, Hook Library, Content Idea Generator, and a Content Calendar to help users plan, create, and organize marketing content.

User privacy and data security are fundamental to the platform. Business accounts operate within their own dedicated data environment, meaning each business retains ownership and control of its information. We do not access, use, or share a business's internal data except where required to provide the services requested by the user. Social media account connections are established securely through each platform's official authorization process, and users can disconnect their accounts or revoke permissions at any time.

12. CONTROLS FOR DO-NOT-TRACK FEATURES

Most web browsers and some mobile operating systems and mobile applications include a Do-Not-Track ('DNT') feature or setting you can activate to signal your privacy preference not to have data about your online browsing activities monitored and collected. At this stage, no uniform technology standard for recognising and implementing DNT signals has been finalised. As such, we do not currently respond to DNT browser signals or any other mechanism that automatically communicates your choice not to be tracked online. If a standard for online tracking is adopted that we must follow in the future, we will inform you about that practice in a revised version of this Privacy Notice.

California law requires us to let you know how we respond to web browser DNT signals. Because there currently is not an industry or legal standard for recognising or honouring DNT signals, we do not respond to them at this time.
